02 · The opportunity

Turn invisible operational leakage into measurable control.

Fixing the workflow is not just a UI improvement. It can reduce disputed time, cut manual follow-up, surface compliance risks earlier and give management a faster path from exception → action.

Operational leverage

Reduce the distance between a problem occurring and management seeing it.

Paper attendance creates room for disputed hours. Manual credential checks consume admin time. Fragmented patient records slow investigations. The platform converts those recurring costs into structured, visible workflows.

Stronger attendance integrity

GPS-assisted clock-in/out, supervisor verification and a logged daily OTP fallback create a clearer audit trail even when location services fail.

Visible compliance status

Automated credential expiry alerts help the office act before licenses or certifications become operational blockers.

One patient documentation trail

Authorized intake, daily charts, incident resolution and appointment management become searchable, permissioned records.

Illustrative payroll exposure

When reported time and actual arrival time do not match, the difference becomes payroll leakage.

Example only—not a claim about Ataam's current payroll. The purpose is to show how even a small amount of overstated time can compound across a field workforce.

$81,250illustrative annual exposure

A worker says 8:00 AM. The facility says 8:15 AM.

If 50 field staff are paid for only 15 minutes more than they actually worked on five shifts per week, at a $25 blended hourly labor cost, that small discrepancy can represent approximately $81,250 over a year. GPS-assisted attendance, supervisor verification and the logged daily OTP fallback are designed to make the paid record match the verified shift record.

07 · Security & compliance

Trust is part of the product.

Because the application handles staff and patient-related records, security cannot be a layer added at the end. It must shape architecture, permissions, logging and deployment from the beginning.

Role-based access control

Permissions reflect clinical responsibility and administrative authority, including restricted patient intake access.

Audit logging

Key record actions, entries and exits are designed to leave traceable system history for operational review.

HIPAA-aligned implementation

Data handling, deployment and access controls will be reviewed against applicable HIPAA safeguards before handoff.

Standalone architecture

Version 1 does not depend on payroll or EHR integrations, reducing external dependencies and keeping scope controlled.

Scope boundary: no historical paper-timesheet migration, native iOS/Android app, payroll/EHR integration, or SMS/push notification service is included in Version 1 unless separately approved through change control.

12 · Commercial terms

Clear boundaries keep the launch fast.

These proposal terms are intentionally readable. Your final service agreement and invoice should contain the complete legal language, payment details and governing terms.

01

Payment

40% commitment fee before project commencement; remaining 60% due at deployment/handoff. Work begins after the first payment and required project inputs are received.

02

Client dependencies

Ataam provides brand assets, intake sheets, timesheets, staff records, intake records, required policies/content, hosting/domain access and a dedicated point of contact for approvals.

03

Feedback & revisions

Feedback should be consolidated through the agreed client point of contact. Revisions are limited to the approved scope and acceptance criteria; new workflows or material feature additions are handled through change control.

04

Scope exclusions

Historical data migration, native mobile apps, payroll/EHR integrations, SMS/push infrastructure and requirements not defined in the approved Version 1 product specification are excluded unless separately quoted.

05

Compliance responsibility

The product will be designed and reviewed with HIPAA requirements in mind, but organisational HIPAA compliance also depends on Ataam’s internal policies, user practices, hosting/provider agreements and operational safeguards outside the software itself.

06

Ownership & handoff

Upon full payment, the agreed production deliverables are handed over to Ataam, including deployment access and project documentation specified in the final agreement. Pre-existing tools, libraries and third-party components remain subject to their original licenses.

07

Timeline assumptions

The 29 September MVP target depends on timely access, approvals and feedback. The wider project is a six-month engagement; client-caused delays, third-party outages or newly requested features can shift dependent milestones or move non-critical work into a later release.

08

Acceptance & agreement

Final scope, payment instructions and legal obligations are confirmed by the executed agreement/invoice. Proposal approval moves the project into contracting and kickoff.
