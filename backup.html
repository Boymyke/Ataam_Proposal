One connected system for every shift.

01 · Understanding of Problem

Paperwork is only the surface problem. The bigger cost is operational uncertainty.

Ataam needs a single controlled system that shows what was scheduled, what actually happened, who is authorised to record care, what is approaching non-compliance and what needs management attention now.

Core understanding

Ataam does not need “another app.” It needs a reliable operating layer for care delivery.

Today, time tracking, staff credentials, patient documentation, incidents and appointments can become separate points of risk. The platform should connect those workflows into one auditable trail.

ScheduledWho was supposed to be there?
VerifiedWho actually arrived and left?
ActionableWhat needs leadership attention?
01Attendance integrity

GPS-assisted clock-in/out, supervisor verification and a logged OTP fallback reduce disputed shift records.

02Credential control

License and certification expiry alerts should surface before they become staffing blockers.

03Care documentation

Patient intake, daily notes, incidents and appointments need one searchable permissioned record.

04Management visibility

Analytics and exception dashboards should show patterns, not force leadership to chase paperwork.

A focused MVP in 6 weeks. Official launch targeted for 7 October 2026.

Following product specification and approval in late August, delivery moves through staff and admin design, frontend and backend completion, QA, client beta, compliance review, priority fixes and production launch.
